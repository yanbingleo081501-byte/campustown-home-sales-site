How the medians were calculated

For each city, sale prices below $1,000 were excluded to remove the implausibly low values flagged in the dataset notes. The median is the middle price after sorting the remaining sales (or the average of the two middle prices when there is an even number of sales). Prices are displayed rounded to the nearest whole dollar; counts show the sales included.

Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026), collected March 2026. Values are as reported by the collection source and are not verified against county records.
